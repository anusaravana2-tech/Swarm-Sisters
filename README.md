Selected Challenge and Solution: Theme A FinSwarm
FinSwarm leverages a 6 agent collaborative AI boardroom comprising Business Research, Finance & Treasury, Credit Risk, 
Marketing & Sales, Compliance & Customer Protection, and a coordinating CEO Agent to dynamically formulate and adapt 
FinNova Capital's digital lending strategy. Using a structured five-stage protocol (Analyse, Share, Challenge, Compare, 
Decide), the swarm balances sustainable capital growth, portfolio default limits (≤5%), regulatory pricing caps (19% APR), 
and liquidity buffers (≥INR 3 crore) across small business segments. Upon receiving mid-pilot operational and market 
shocks such as credit risk spikes, marketing budget cuts, enhanced verification bottlenecks, and elevated funding/fraud 
costs the multi-agent workflow re-runs impacted department nodes to instantly reallocate acquisition channels, tighten 
approval policies, deploy automated verification tools, and re-balance the segment portfolio while preserving target 
returns and compliance boundaries.
